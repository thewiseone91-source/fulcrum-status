    How to read this page

    A timer on the Tower checks its services once a minute and publishes the result with a UTC timestamp. The timer only runs while the Tower is running Linux and can reach the internet.

    OPERATIONAL: a recent heartbeat, every component up. DEGRADED: a recent heartbeat, at least one component down; the list shows which.

    OFFLINE / WINDOWS MODE: no heartbeat for more than five minutes. The Tower is powered off, booted into Windows, or cut off from the internet. This page cannot tell those apart; in all three the app, the Sparks and the dev tunnel are unreachable.

    STATUS UNKNOWN: this page could not get a trustworthy copy of the heartbeat. It says nothing about the Tower.

    "Dev tunnel endpoint" is checked from the Tower's side only. It does not prove that the tunnel is reachable from where you are.
